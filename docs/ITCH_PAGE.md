## Description

> **Five colours. Five lanes. One crystal.**
>
> A crystal stands at the heart of a pentagon. Five lanes lead to it, one for each colour of
> mana - White, Blue, Black, Red and Green - and every wave the colours march their warbands
> down them: melee screens in front, archers behind, mages at the core. Neighbouring colours
> ally and charge as one.
>
> Pick one of five classes - Warrior, Knight, Barbarian, Archer or Mage - and fight in third
> person with its weapon and the spells of the colours you choose. Everyone levels together;
> between waves the team spends its shared mana at Upkeep. If the crystal falls, the run is over.
>
> **Features**
>
> - Co-op for one to five players - host, join, or play solo.
> - Five classes, seventy skills across five colours, each with five ranks.
> - A tower for every colour, from the Ivory Tower's shields to the Hornet Nest.
> - Myrs - little workers that harvest the mana wells for the team. They run from danger,
>   behind a Myr Enforcer or a Lodestone Myr if their lane has one, and a fallen one can be
>   brought back from the Graveyard at its level.
> - A boss every fifth wave - the Paladin, Frost Giant, Zombie Lord, Fire Giant and Treant,
>   three phases each, every attack marked on the ground first.
> - Day and night: the nights are dark, and you see by the crystal's light and your spells.
>
> **Install**
>
> Install it through the itch app and it updates itself - each new version downloads only
> what changed. Windows, 64-bit.
>
> *This is a work in progress, shared with testers.*
